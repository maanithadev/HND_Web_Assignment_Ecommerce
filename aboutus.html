About Lakshan Flora

Lakshan Flora, founded in 2022 by Lakshan Alwis, is a family-owned business specializing in wedding flowers, gift flowers, corporate arrangements, home décor, and sympathy flowers. From simple buttonhole flowers to elegant wedding or church decorations, we offer beautiful, stylish designs for every occasion.

With locations in Dalugama, Wattala, Mabola, Ja-Ela, and Gampaha, as well as online services, we make quality floral arrangements accessible to everyone. Our commitment to excellence and affordability has driven our growth, and we strive to make every moment special with our creations.

Our Vision

To become a leading florist known for our artistic and sustainable floral designs, bringing beauty to every moment in every life event to anyneeds.

Our Mission

Our mission is to provide top-quality floral arrangements, exceptional customer service, and unique designs that bring joy to our clients' lives.
